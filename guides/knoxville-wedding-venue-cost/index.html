How much does a wedding venue cost in Knoxville, TN?

Short answer: published Knoxville venue fees run from about $150 (Marble Springs) to $12,950 (Marblegate Farm). The average Knoxville wedding cost $28,380 in 2025 and the median was $16,592 (The Wedding Report).

What does the average Knoxville wedding cost?

The Wedding Report estimates the 2025 average at $28,380 and the median at $16,592, across 5,194 Knoxville-market weddings.

How much do Knoxville couples spend on the venue?

The Wedding Report's 125-guest Knoxville estimate puts venue, catering and rentals at $11,141–$13,617, about 48% of the budget.

What is the cheapest Knoxville wedding venue with published prices?

Marble Springs State Historic Site lists rental fees from $150 to $500 for three hours.
