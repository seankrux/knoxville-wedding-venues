UT Gardens Knoxville

The State Botanical Garden of Tennessee, with outdoor ceremony spots including the Beall Family Rose Garden and McIlwaine Friendship Pavilion.

  • Area: University of Tennessee campus (2518 Jacob Dr)
  • Capacity: Holly Room: 150 theater-style / 100 at rounds
  • Style: garden, outdoor, budget
  • Price: Wedding package (McIlwaine Pavilion + Rose Garden) $1,200 for 4 hours; garden spaces from $250 for 2 hours (2024 rate sheet)
  • Official website: https://utgardens.tennessee.edu/locations/knoxville/event-space-knoxville/the-holly-room/
