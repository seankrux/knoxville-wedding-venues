Maple Grove Estate

Historic 10,000 sq ft private estate on 15.5 acres off Westland Drive, originally built in 1799 as the home of Reverend Samuel Graham Ramsey and known over the years as Glenmary, Mount Ebenezer and the Parsonage. The house mixes Georgian and Federal architecture; it was a private residence until 1993, then a bed and breakfast, and was renovated in 2018. The Base Wedding Package includes 9 hours of exclusive property rental (4 hours of event time) plus 1 hour of rehearsal time; a choice of ceremony and reception locations for up to 185 guests; 300 fruitwood garden chairs and round, rectangular, farmhouse and cocktail tables; a 600 sq ft Bridal Suite (covered porch, fireplace lounge, private terrace entrance and bathroom) and a 600 sq ft Groom's Quarters (fireplace lounge, private bathroom); parking for up to 100 vehicles; a pre-approved vendor list; and grounds access for engagement photos. Add-ons include extended event time, arbor rentals and a Rehearsal Dinner in the Banquet Room (4 hours for up to 50 guests, with a service kitchen for licensed and insured caterers). No price is published.
