Marblegate Farm

Private riverfront peninsula with the 7,200 sq ft climate-controlled Marblegate Hall, a farmhouse and a boathouse; about 25 minutes from downtown Knoxville.

  • Area: Friendsville, TN (308 McReynolds Rd, Tennessee River)
  • Capacity: 50–250 in Marblegate Hall (call for 250+)
  • Style: barn, farm, lakefront, outdoor
  • Price: Rental $6,950–$12,950
  • Official website: https://marblegatefarm.com/
