The Quarry Venue

Family-run venue in the historic Bond Quarry, which produced "Bond Pink" Tennessee marble. Spaces include The Canyon (a partially filled quarry with rock walls and a 6,000 sq ft patio beside a pond and waterfall, suited to tented events), The Carrara (a 6,000 sq ft industrial steel building for up to 300 with views of a 100-ft-plus waterfall) and The Terrazzo (an 1,800 sq ft 1930s building made from quarry marble, for rehearsal dinners). About 10–15 minutes from downtown, McGhee Tyson Airport and Turkey Creek.

  • Area: West Knoxville (2320 Keller Bend Rd)
  • Capacity: Up to 300 (The Carrara, 6,000 sq ft)
  • Style: unique, outdoor, historic, industrial
  • Price: Not published; ask the venue
  • Official website: https://thequarryvenue.com/
