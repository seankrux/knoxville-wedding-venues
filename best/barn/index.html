Best Barn wedding venues in Knoxville, TN

8 venues.

RiverView Family Farm

West Knoxville (12130 Prater Ln, Fort Loudon Lake) · Up to 200 (packages include 150; $18 per additional guest) · barn, rustic, lakefront, farm

Working cattle farm since 1801 with a 6,000 sq ft gambrel Legacy Barn built in 2014 from farm-milled timber. Packages (Reception, or Ceremony & Reception with a 1-hour rehearsal and a 2-hour bridal or engagement photo session) include a 3-hour reception for up to 150 guests, farm tables and chairs, the Farmhouse for bridal party dressing, a reception coordinator and ceremony director, a catering prep area, and on-site event and security staff. Base package prices are not published; Friday and Sunday events are discounted. Tours by appointment.

Grand Valley Farms

South Knoxville (1607 Grand Valley Rd), on the French Broad River · Up to 150 · farm, barn, outdoor, lakefront

Working farm and indoor/outdoor event venue on 6.5 acres on the French Broad River, hand-cleared by the owners, for small groups up to 150 guests. One event per weekend, with use of the entire venue; reception-only rental is available, there is no Friday discount, and active-military couples get $500 off. The covered barn, which has doors that close, is the included rain plan at no extra charge. Amenities: full kitchen, 20 ft bar on caster wheels, adjustable/dimmable lighting, wired speakers, outdoor lighting, rock terrace, bonfire pit, lawn games, Wi-Fi, two bathrooms with showers and on-site parking (overnight parking with notice). No planning services: a professional wedding planner or coordinator is required. Tables, chairs and décor come from outside vendors; portable restrooms are required above 50 guests; alcohol is allowed (BYO, not provided); amplified music ends at 9 PM; wedding liability insurance is required; not ADA compliant. About 12 minutes from downtown, 5 minutes from Chapman Highway and 20 minutes from McGhee Tyson Airport. Pricing is only in a pamphlet sent on inquiry, so none is listed.

The Stables at Strawberry Creek

Northeast Knoxville (733 McCubbins Rd) · Up to 300 (250+ indoors for rain) · barn, rustic, all-inclusive, farm

55-acre all-inclusive venue with a historic bridal log cabin and overnight lodging.

Heartland Meadows

Knoxville area (5428 Salem Church Rd) · Up to 300 (ceremony sites and main venue seating) · barn, rustic, outdoor, budget

Family-owned 58-acre outdoor venue with multiple ceremony sites (the Old Oak Tree with a 250-year-old oak and seating up to 300, the Tree Deck, the Cove for intimate weddings, and the Rustic Ceremony Barn as an indoor rain plan), a Reception Barn, all-day Bridal Suite and Groom's Quarters with private bathrooms, rustic farm tables, padded garden chairs and church pews, Porta Cools and patio heaters, and facility use from 10am–10pm. Complimentary Treasure Barn of décor and props, open vendor policy (including food trucks), pet-friendly, and complimentary engagement-photo access. Alcohol is permitted with a licensed and insured bartender. No published base rental price.

Cedar Pond Farms

New Market, TN (1369 Collins Rd), in the Smoky foothills near Sevierville, Knoxville and Gatlinburg · Up to 250 (Day Wedding up to 100, expandable to 250; Elopement up to 25) · barn, farm, outdoor, budget

31.5-acre working Scottish Highland cattle farm (a 1950s dairy, home to the Wilmoth family since 2004). Only one wedding on the farm per day, and all of its more than a dozen outdoor ceremony sites are included. The Grand White Barn has ready rooms, a catering kitchen and accessible restrooms, and the covered Grand Veranda is the rain plan. Packages: the Elopement (up to 25 guests, four-hour window) and the Day Wedding (up to 100, expandable to 250), each with an all-inclusive add-on (catering, florals, DJ, planning) and a whole-farm buyout option. Guests can stay overnight in on-site barndominiums and Farm Stay houses; a Highland cow cocktail hour and an on-site gelato cart are offered as extras. No price is published.

Pure Water Farm

Walland, TN (501 West Millers Cove Rd) · 100 in the barn; 200+ with a tent · barn, rustic, mountain, outdoor

Purpose-built event barn (built by local craftsmen for events, never used for farming) with a schoolhouse and barn restored from an earlier European settlement on the property. The barn has aged wood siding, hand-cut beams, archways, a hand-built bar and heated floors, and holds up to 100 guests for a plated or family-style dinner with a dance floor; a tent by the barn entrance allows 200+ guests. Ceremonies can be outdoors under a flowered arch, surrounded by the Smoky Mountains, or indoors in the barn. The wedding rental package includes one year of wedding planning. About 15 minutes from McGhee Tyson Airport, 10 minutes from Maryville and 20 minutes from Knoxville.

Marblegate Farm

Friendsville, TN (308 McReynolds Rd, Tennessee River) · 50–250 in Marblegate Hall (call for 250+) · barn, farm, lakefront, outdoor

Private riverfront peninsula with the 7,200 sq ft climate-controlled Marblegate Hall, a farmhouse and a boathouse; about 25 minutes from downtown Knoxville.

The Barn Event Center of the Smokies

Townsend, TN (7264 E Lamar Alexander Pkwy) · Up to 200 · mountain, barn, garden

Cantilever barn and award-winning gardens near Great Smoky Mountains National Park and the Foothills Parkway, with a great room, built-in stage, onsite catering and a bridal suite.
