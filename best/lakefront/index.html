Best Lakefront and riverfront wedding venues in Knoxville, TN

3 venues.

The Pavilion at Hunter Valley Farm

West Knoxville (9133 Hunter Valley Ln) · Amphitheater ceremony seating up to 350; elopement package up to 30 · lakefront, outdoor

Lakefront venue on Bluegrass Lake in West Knoxville with 6,000 sq ft of heated and cooled event space, bride and groom suites, a caterer's kitchen, and a waterside outdoor amphitheater reached by a boardwalk.

RiverView Family Farm

West Knoxville (12130 Prater Ln, Fort Loudon Lake) · Up to 200 (packages include 150; $18 per additional guest) · barn, rustic, lakefront, farm

Working cattle farm since 1801 with a 6,000 sq ft gambrel Legacy Barn built in 2014 from farm-milled timber. Packages (Reception, or Ceremony & Reception with a 1-hour rehearsal and a 2-hour bridal or engagement photo session) include a 3-hour reception for up to 150 guests, farm tables and chairs, the Farmhouse for bridal party dressing, a reception coordinator and ceremony director, a catering prep area, and on-site event and security staff. Base package prices are not published; Friday and Sunday events are discounted. Tours by appointment.

Marblegate Farm

Friendsville, TN (308 McReynolds Rd, Tennessee River) · 50–250 in Marblegate Hall (call for 250+) · barn, farm, lakefront, outdoor

Private riverfront peninsula with the 7,200 sq ft climate-controlled Marblegate Hall, a farmhouse and a boathouse; about 25 minutes from downtown Knoxville.
