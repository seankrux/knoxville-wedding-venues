Best Smoky Mountain and foothills wedding venues in Knoxville, TN

Venues in Townsend, Walland, Maryville, New Market and other foothill towns within about an hour of Knoxville.

6 venues.

Cedar Pond Farms

New Market, TN (1369 Collins Rd) · Up to 250 · barn, farm, outdoor, budget

Working farm with Scottish Highland cows, a Grand White Barn, over a dozen ceremony sites and an elopement package for 25.

Pure Water Farm

Near Maryville, TN · 100 in the barn; 200+ with a tent · barn, rustic, mountain

Purpose-built event barn with heated floors, 15 minutes from McGhee Tyson Airport.

Dancing Bear Lodge & Appalachian Bistro

Townsend, TN (Smoky Mountain foothills) · Outdoor ceremony up to 200; pavilion dining up to 75 · mountain, outdoor, rustic, intimate

Smoky Mountain lodge and bistro with an open-air pavilion, an event room and an outdoor ceremony space with a gazebo.

Blackberry Farm

Walland, TN (1471 West Millers Cove Rd) · Up to 140 guests (Bramble Hall / overnight packages) · mountain, estate, elegant

4,200-acre Relais & Châteaux estate in the foothills of the Smokies; Bramble Hall hosts events up to 140, and wedding packages house guests in rooms, cottages and homes.

Tremont Lodge & Resort

Townsend, TN (7726 E Lamar Alexander Pkwy) · Up to 240 · mountain, large

Two-level event lodge minutes from Great Smoky Mountains National Park, with indoor and outdoor spaces, all-inclusive package options, and up to 130 rooms and suites on site.

The Barn Event Center of the Smokies

Townsend, TN (7264 E Lamar Alexander Pkwy) · Up to 200 · mountain, barn, garden

Cantilever barn and award-winning gardens near Great Smoky Mountains National Park and the Foothills Parkway, with a great room, built-in stage, onsite catering and a bridal suite.
