Best Wedding venues for 250+ guests in Knoxville, TN

16 venues.

The Mill & Mine

Downtown Knoxville (227 W Depot Ave) · Up to 450 seated · industrial, downtown, large

Concert venue that also books weddings: 8,000 sq ft indoor space, an 8,000 sq ft courtyard lawn and dressing rooms. In-house catering and bar, and a professional wedding planner is required.

The Relix

Happy Holler, North Knoxville · 150 seated / 250 standing · industrial, downtown, intimate

3,000 sq ft modern venue in the former CI Vest Dry Goods building with exposed brick and 16-ft wood ceilings.

The Brookside

Happy Holler, North Knoxville (1221 N Central St) · 300 seated / 400 standing · downtown, elegant, large

Two-floor elegant venue with a chandeliered Grand Ballroom, a lower lounge and a front patio; sister venue to The Relix.

On Broadway Event Center

North Knoxville (4683 Old Broadway) · Up to 250 guests (about 5,000 sq ft) · industrial, all-inclusive

Built in 1952 as the Suburban Ford dealership and renovated as an event venue, with a glass-walled showroom, garage bay windows, a 12 x 16 ft stage, a bridal suite and a private patio. In-house catering and bar are required.

The Press Room

North Knoxville / edge of downtown (730 N Broadway) · Up to 600 (The Assembly, 10,000 sq ft) · downtown, historic, large, all-inclusive, outdoor

Historic 1923 building (once a bowling alley, then an automobile showroom, then a print shop) with a mostly glass facade, old brick walls and original steel trusses. Spaces include The Assembly (10,000 sq ft, up to 600 guests), the 1923 Loft (800 sq ft, up to 30), the Printer's Lounge (900 sq ft) and a 3,200 sq ft Courtyard. All-inclusive weddings cover catering through City Catering, décor, setup, lighting, entertainment, staffing, bar service and day-of coordination, with allowances for florals, linens, cake and place settings; tables, wooden crossback chairs, flatware, glassware, chargers and china are included.

Knoxville Museum of Art

World's Fair Park, Downtown · 200 seated / 250 standing (Bailey Hall) · downtown, garden, modern

Ann and Steve Bailey Hall plus the museum gardens; tables and chairs included.

The Pavilion at Hunter Valley Farm

West Knoxville (9133 Hunter Valley Ln) · Amphitheater ceremony seating up to 350; elopement package up to 30 · lakefront, outdoor

Lakefront venue on Bluegrass Lake in West Knoxville with 6,000 sq ft of heated and cooled event space, bride and groom suites, a caterer's kitchen, and a waterside outdoor amphitheater reached by a boardwalk.

The Stables at Strawberry Creek

Northeast Knoxville (733 McCubbins Rd) · Up to 300 (250+ indoors for rain) · barn, rustic, all-inclusive, farm

55-acre all-inclusive venue with a historic bridal log cabin and overnight lodging.

The Grand Victorian

East Tennessee hills near Knoxville · Up to 250 outdoors · rustic, garden, outdoor

Family-owned venue in the rolling hills of East Tennessee with two fountain ponds, a gazebo overlooking a pond, a waterfall, a wooded area and a white carriage house. Four ceremony sites, with indoor and outdoor options for both ceremony and reception. No in-house catering; the venue keeps a preferred vendor list. Pricing is in a pamphlet sent on inquiry, not published on the site.

The Quarry Venue

West Knoxville (2320 Keller Bend Rd) · Up to 300 (The Carrara, 6,000 sq ft) · unique, outdoor, historic, industrial

Family-run venue in the historic Bond Quarry, which produced "Bond Pink" Tennessee marble. Spaces include The Canyon (a partially filled quarry with rock walls and a 6,000 sq ft patio beside a pond and waterfall, suited to tented events), The Carrara (a 6,000 sq ft industrial steel building for up to 300 with views of a 100-ft-plus waterfall) and The Terrazzo (an 1,800 sq ft 1930s building made from quarry marble, for rehearsal dinners). About 10–15 minutes from downtown, McGhee Tyson Airport and Turkey Creek.

Heartland Meadows

Knoxville area (5428 Salem Church Rd) · Up to 300 (ceremony sites and main venue seating) · barn, rustic, outdoor, budget

Family-owned 58-acre outdoor venue with multiple ceremony sites (the Old Oak Tree with a 250-year-old oak and seating up to 300, the Tree Deck, the Cove for intimate weddings, and the Rustic Ceremony Barn as an indoor rain plan), a Reception Barn, all-day Bridal Suite and Groom's Quarters with private bathrooms, rustic farm tables, padded garden chairs and church pews, Porta Cools and patio heaters, and facility use from 10am–10pm. Complimentary Treasure Barn of décor and props, open vendor policy (including food trucks), pet-friendly, and complimentary engagement-photo access. Alcohol is permitted with a licensed and insured bartender. No published base rental price.

Cedar Pond Farms

New Market, TN (1369 Collins Rd) · Up to 250 · barn, farm, outdoor, budget

Working farm with Scottish Highland cows, a Grand White Barn, over a dozen ceremony sites and an elopement package for 25.

Marblegate Farm

Friendsville, TN (308 McReynolds Rd, Tennessee River) · 50–250 in Marblegate Hall (call for 250+) · barn, farm, lakefront, outdoor

Private riverfront peninsula with the 7,200 sq ft climate-controlled Marblegate Hall, a farmhouse and a boathouse; about 25 minutes from downtown Knoxville.

Knoxville Botanical Garden and Arboretum

East Knoxville · Dogwood Center: 250 ceremony / 200 reception · garden, outdoor

Garden venue with the award-winning Dogwood Center, Stone Terraces, an ivy-covered Stone Greenhouse and a meadow facing the Smokies.

Historic Ramsey House

East Knox County · Up to 300 outdoors; 80 indoors · historic, outdoor, budget

1797 stone house on 100 acres; ceremonies are outdoors, with an indoor reception room for up to 80.

Tremont Lodge & Resort

Townsend, TN (7726 E Lamar Alexander Pkwy) · Up to 240 · mountain, large

Two-level event lodge minutes from Great Smoky Mountains National Park, with indoor and outdoor spaces, all-inclusive package options, and up to 130 rooms and suites on site.
