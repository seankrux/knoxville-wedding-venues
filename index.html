The Mill & Mine

Downtown Knoxville (227 W Depot Ave) · Up to 450 seated · industrial, downtown, large

Concert venue that also books weddings: 8,000 sq ft indoor space, an 8,000 sq ft courtyard lawn and dressing rooms. In-house catering and bar, and a professional wedding planner is required.

The Standard

Old City, Knoxville (416 W Jackson Ave) · Up to 140 guests for weddings (220 for other events) · industrial, historic, downtown

Restored historic Old City building with about 10,000 sq ft of usable space, exposed brick and skylights, a rooftop patio, a bridal suite and a groomsman lounge, within walking distance of downtown restaurants and hotels. Catering is available through City Catering.

The Relix

Happy Holler, North Knoxville · 150 seated / 250 standing · industrial, downtown, intimate

3,000 sq ft modern venue in the former CI Vest Dry Goods building with exposed brick and 16-ft wood ceilings.

The Brookside

Happy Holler, North Knoxville (1221 N Central St) · 300 seated / 400 standing · downtown, elegant, large

Two-floor elegant venue with a chandeliered Grand Ballroom, a lower lounge and a front patio; sister venue to The Relix.

On Broadway Event Center

North Knoxville (4683 Old Broadway) · Up to 250 guests (about 5,000 sq ft) · industrial, all-inclusive

Built in 1952 as the Suburban Ford dealership and renovated as an event venue, with a glass-walled showroom, garage bay windows, a 12 x 16 ft stage, a bridal suite and a private patio. In-house catering and bar are required.

Five Thirty Lounge

Downtown Knoxville (530 S Gay St) · Capacity not published · rooftop, downtown, intimate

Rooftop lounge 10 stories up at the Hyatt Place (former Historic Farragut Hotel), surrounded by 15-ft glass with views of downtown and the Smoky Mountains; private parties available.

The Adelene

Fountain City, North Knoxville (104 Hotel Rd) · Capacity not published · intimate

Naturally lit 2,500 sq ft event and studio space in a historic Fountain City building, with two rooms (the bright Broadway Room and the moody Hotel Room); event rentals include the whole space.

The Press Room

Downtown Knoxville · Up to 600 indoors · downtown, historic, large

Historic downtown venue with multiple flexible spaces; outdoor area holds 125 for ceremonies.

Knoxville Museum of Art

World's Fair Park, Downtown · 200 seated / 250 standing (Bailey Hall) · downtown, garden, modern

Ann and Steve Bailey Hall plus the museum gardens; tables and chairs included.

Historic Bleak House

West Knoxville (3148 Kingston Pike) · About 150 for a reception and 75 for a sit-down meal in the house; more with the terrace and gardens · historic, garden, outdoor, budget

About 10,000 sq ft mansion built circa 1858 on Kingston Pike, overlooking a bend of the Tennessee River, with over three acres of five-level terraced gardens. Couples can use much of the house for dressing and photos; a traffic and security officer is provided. No all-inclusive packages.

The Pavilion at Hunter Valley Farm

West Knoxville (9133 Hunter Valley Ln) · Amphitheater ceremony seating up to 350; elopement package up to 30 · lakefront, outdoor

Lakefront venue on Bluegrass Lake in West Knoxville with 6,000 sq ft of heated and cooled event space, bride and groom suites, a caterer's kitchen, and a waterside outdoor amphitheater reached by a boardwalk.

RiverView Family Farm

West Knoxville (12130 Prater Ln, Fort Loudon Lake) · Up to 200 (packages include 150; $18 per additional guest) · barn, rustic, lakefront, farm

Working cattle farm since 1801 with a 6,000 sq ft gambrel Legacy Barn built in 2014 from farm-milled timber. Packages (Reception, or Ceremony & Reception with a 1-hour rehearsal and a 2-hour bridal or engagement photo session) include a 3-hour reception for up to 150 guests, farm tables and chairs, the Farmhouse for bridal party dressing, a reception coordinator and ceremony director, a catering prep area, and on-site event and security staff. Base package prices are not published; Friday and Sunday events are discounted. Tours by appointment.

Grand Valley Farms

South Knoxville (1607 Grand Valley Rd) · Up to 150 · farm, outdoor

South Knoxville event venue for weddings, 15 minutes from downtown by boat or car and 20 minutes from McGhee Tyson Airport.

The Stables at Strawberry Creek

Northeast Knoxville (733 McCubbins Rd) · Up to 300 (250+ indoors for rain) · barn, rustic, all-inclusive, farm

55-acre all-inclusive venue with a historic bridal log cabin and overnight lodging.

The Julianna Wedding & Event Venue

West Knoxville (9111 Hunter Valley Ln) · Garden ceremony seating up to 150 · elegant, garden, estate

Family-owned estate on over 32 acres of farmland a short drive from West Knoxville. The garden ceremony site has a wooden arch on a stone platform with room for up to 150 chairs. The 6,000 sq ft Reception Hall, renovated in 2020 from the original stables, has crystal chandeliers, a dual-sided fireplace opening to the Courtyard, a Bridal Suite and a Groom's Lounge, and doubles as the rain plan for outdoor ceremonies. A farmhouse on the property sleeps up to 10 guests.

The Grand Victorian

East Tennessee hills near Knoxville · Up to 250 outdoors · rustic, garden, outdoor

Family-owned venue in the rolling hills of East Tennessee with two fountain ponds, a gazebo overlooking a pond, a waterfall, a wooded area and a white carriage house. Four ceremony sites, with indoor and outdoor options for both ceremony and reception. No in-house catering; the venue keeps a preferred vendor list. Pricing is in a pamphlet sent on inquiry, not published on the site.

The Manor at Hickory Creek

West Knoxville (12544 Yarnell Rd) · Up to 110 · intimate, budget, outdoor

6-acre creekside venue with indoor and outdoor spaces and on-site lodging for 16 (5 bedrooms).

The Quarry Venue

West Knoxville (2320 Keller Bend Rd) · Up to 300 (The Carrara, 6,000 sq ft) · unique, outdoor, historic, industrial

Family-run venue in the historic Bond Quarry, which produced "Bond Pink" Tennessee marble. Spaces include The Canyon (a partially filled quarry with rock walls and a 6,000 sq ft patio beside a pond and waterfall, suited to tented events), The Carrara (a 6,000 sq ft industrial steel building for up to 300 with views of a 100-ft-plus waterfall) and The Terrazzo (an 1,800 sq ft 1930s building made from quarry marble, for rehearsal dinners). About 10–15 minutes from downtown, McGhee Tyson Airport and Turkey Creek.

Heartland Meadows

East Tennessee (Knoxville area) · Ceremony site seats up to 300 · barn, rustic, outdoor, budget

58-acre outdoor venue with a 250-year-old oak ceremony site and a free-to-use 'Treasure Barn' of decor.

Cedar Pond Farms

New Market, TN (1369 Collins Rd) · Up to 250 · barn, farm, outdoor, budget

Working farm with Scottish Highland cows, a Grand White Barn, over a dozen ceremony sites and an elopement package for 25.

Pure Water Farm

Near Maryville, TN · 100 in the barn; 200+ with a tent · barn, rustic, mountain

Purpose-built event barn with heated floors, 15 minutes from McGhee Tyson Airport.

Marblegate Farm

Friendsville, TN (308 McReynolds Rd, Tennessee River) · 50–250 in Marblegate Hall (call for 250+) · barn, farm, lakefront, outdoor

Private riverfront peninsula with the 7,200 sq ft climate-controlled Marblegate Hall, a farmhouse and a boathouse; about 25 minutes from downtown Knoxville.

UT Gardens Knoxville

University of Tennessee campus (2518 Jacob Dr) · Holly Room: 150 theater-style / 100 at rounds · garden, outdoor, budget

The State Botanical Garden of Tennessee, with outdoor ceremony spots including the Beall Family Rose Garden and McIlwaine Friendship Pavilion.

Maple Grove Estate

West Knoxville (8800 Westland Dr) · Capacity not published · estate, historic, garden

Historic 10,000 sq ft private estate on over 15 acres off Westland Drive, originally built in 1799. Offers a Base Wedding Package with property rental, bridal suite and groom's quarters.

Knoxville Botanical Garden and Arboretum

East Knoxville · Dogwood Center: 250 ceremony / 200 reception · garden, outdoor

Garden venue with the award-winning Dogwood Center, Stone Terraces, an ivy-covered Stone Greenhouse and a meadow facing the Smokies.

Marble Springs State Historic Site

South Knoxville (1220 W Gov John Sevier Hwy) · 150+ guest pricing tier · historic, outdoor, budget

State-owned historic site with woodlands and meadows; very low published wedding rental fees.

Historic Ramsey House

East Knox County · Up to 300 outdoors; 80 indoors · historic, outdoor, budget

1797 stone house on 100 acres; ceremonies are outdoors, with an indoor reception room for up to 80.

Castleton Estate

Loudon, TN · Up to 200 (Saturdays); 125 on select Sundays/weekdays · estate, garden, elegant, all-inclusive

110-acre gated estate with an antebellum-style manor, a restored equestrian Carriage House and Tuscan vineyard gardens; one wedding per day.

Dancing Bear Lodge & Appalachian Bistro

Townsend, TN (Smoky Mountain foothills) · Outdoor ceremony up to 200; pavilion dining up to 75 · mountain, outdoor, rustic, intimate

Smoky Mountain lodge and bistro with an open-air pavilion, an event room and an outdoor ceremony space with a gazebo.

Blackberry Farm

Walland, TN (1471 West Millers Cove Rd) · Up to 140 guests (Bramble Hall / overnight packages) · mountain, estate, elegant

4,200-acre Relais & Châteaux estate in the foothills of the Smokies; Bramble Hall hosts events up to 140, and wedding packages house guests in rooms, cottages and homes.

Tremont Lodge & Resort

Townsend, TN (7726 E Lamar Alexander Pkwy) · Up to 240 · mountain, large

Two-level event lodge minutes from Great Smoky Mountains National Park, with indoor and outdoor spaces, all-inclusive package options, and up to 130 rooms and suites on site.

The Barn Event Center of the Smokies

Townsend, TN (7264 E Lamar Alexander Pkwy) · Up to 200 · mountain, barn, garden

Cantilever barn and award-winning gardens near Great Smoky Mountains National Park and the Foothills Parkway, with a great room, built-in stage, onsite catering and a bridal suite.
