Knoxville Wedding Venue FAQ

How many weddings happen in Knoxville each year?

The Wedding Report estimates 5,194 weddings in the Knoxville market in 2025, with an average cost of $28,380 and a median of $16,592 (wedding.report).

How much does a Knoxville wedding venue cost?

Published examples from venue sites: Knoxville Museum of Art $6,000–$7,500 for a 4-hour wedding; The Manor at Hickory Creek elopements from $2,500 weekdays / $3,500 weekends and packages $3,900–$7,500; The Stables at Strawberry Creek fall 2026 special $2,999 (valid only for remaining Oct–Nov 2026 dates; checked Oct 14, 2026; regular pricing not published); UT Gardens wedding package $1,200; Marble Springs $150–$500; Historic Ramsey House $700–$2,000. Most venues do not publish prices.

How much is a marriage license in Knox County?

$97.50, reduced to $37.50 with a premarital preparation course. It is valid for 30 days (Knox County Clerk).

Which Knoxville venues hold 300+ guests?

From capacities each venue publishes: The Press Room (up to 600 indoors), The Mill & Mine (up to 450 seated), The Brookside (300 seated / 400 standing), The Stables at Strawberry Creek (up to 300), Heartland Meadows (up to 300), and Historic Ramsey House (up to 300 outdoors). Confirm current limits with the venue.

Where can I find Smoky Mountain foothills wedding venues near Knoxville?

Venues in Townsend, Walland, Maryville and New Market within about an hour of Knoxville are listed on the mountain category page: Dancing Bear Lodge & Appalachian Bistro, Blackberry Farm, Tremont Lodge & Resort, The Barn Event Center of the Smokies, Pure Water Farm and Cedar Pond Farms.

Which Knoxville venues are garden or botanical wedding spots?

Published garden options include UT Gardens Knoxville (Beall Family Rose Garden and McIlwaine Friendship Pavilion; wedding package $1,200), Knoxville Botanical Garden and Arboretum (Dogwood Center 250 ceremony / 200 reception; private-event rates $2,000–$7,000), Historic Bleak House (terraced gardens to the Tennessee River), Maple Grove Estate (historic estate gardens off Westland Drive), and The Julianna (garden and refurbished stables for ceremonies). Confirm current availability with each venue.
